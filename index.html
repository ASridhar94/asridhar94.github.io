About

From the lab bench to the boardroom — and everywhere between.

My career began at the molecular level — elbow-deep in DNA extractions across India, Sweden, and Canada, decoding the genomic logic behind drug resistance and infectious disease. That scientific rigour taught me something irreplaceable: every complex problem has an underlying structure you can decode if you ask the right questions.

But science alone wasn't enough. I wanted to build things that scale.

Today I co-found, lead product, and shape strategy at Travel CarePlans and HEAL Academy — two ventures reshaping how healthcare professionals learn and how travellers navigate complex health systems. I've launched across 5 international markets, managed $2M+ regulated initiatives, and driven 300% growth in customer acquisition.

I speak fluently across engineering, clinical, and business teams — translating between worlds is my competitive advantage. Like chess, I see the full board. Like basketball, I move fast and adapt faster.
