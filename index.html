01 — About

From Lab Bench to Boardroom: Building at the Intersection of Science, Strategy & Systems

I am a product strategist, systems thinker, and builder — someone who finds the same satisfaction in deconstructing a genomic dataset as I do in reading a chessboard three moves ahead or calling a play on the basketball court. Strategy, precision, and adaptability are not just skills I've cultivated — they are how I operate in every arena.

My career began at the molecular level. Armed with a background spanning genomics, bioinformatics, and molecular biology across India, Sweden, and Canada, I spent years translating complex biological data into public health insights. I built pipelines, designed surveillance protocols, and developed tools used across provincial healthcare networks — work that reached over five million people. That scientific rigor taught me something invaluable: every problem, no matter how complex, has an underlying logic you can decode if you ask the right questions.

But science alone wasn't enough. I wanted to build things that scale.

That drive led me to pivot from research into product and program leadership — a move that required the same courage as a bold chess opening: calculated, deliberate, and full of conviction. I walked away from a PhD not because I failed, but because I chose a bigger board. I stopped measuring success by others' metrics and started building by my own. That decision unlocked everything that followed.

Today, I lead product and education strategy at HEAL Academy and Travel CarePlans — two tech-enabled ventures reshaping how healthcare professionals learn and how senior travellers navigate the system globally. I have taken products from zero to launch across five international markets, driven 300% growth in customer acquisition, and managed $2M+ regulated initiatives with compliance across HIPAA, PIPEDA, and GDPR. I speak fluently across engineering, clinical, and business functions — translating between worlds is my competitive advantage.

Like chess, I see the full board. Like basketball, I move fast, adapt faster, and know when to drive and when to pass.

I founded initiatives, wrote grants that turned ideas into funded programs, organized multi-institute research conferences, and helped build community-first events from the ground up. Every initiative I have touched has been grounded in one belief: the best systems are built for people, not just metrics.

I am not interested in incremental. I build for impact, scale, and legacy.

And the most important player I compete against — every single day — is the version of myself from yesterday.
